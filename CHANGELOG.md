# Changelog

## 0.6.0

**New**
- Test in game: one click saves and starts OpenMW in the cell you are editing, standing where you choose.
- New desktop layout: toolbar, left panel, inspector and key hints.
- Room builder: build interiors on a grid from a theme's walls, floors and doors, or start from a ready-made blueprint (house, tavern, chapel, hut).
- Your own blueprints: save a selection or a whole cell, place it anywhere, in the room builder too.
- Blueprints window: browse them with a 3D preview, place or delete them.
- A furniture blueprint is placed as a group: one click selects all of it.
- Drag a box to select several objects (Shift + drag in the room builder).
- Add many: keep placing an object, with a see-through preview.
- Create potions, ingredients, lights, statics, activators, doors and containers.
- Edit enchantments and spells.
- Records window: everything your project created or changed.

**Improved**
- Snap to grid and Snap to steps in a box at the top left of the view.
- Model previews when choosing a model; lists load more as you scroll.
- A group keeps its shape when moved with Snap to grid.
- Clearer wording throughout.

**Fixed**
- New lights now show under Simulate lighting.
- Pickers show your version of a changed enchantment or spell.
- A new item no longer disappears right after being placed.
